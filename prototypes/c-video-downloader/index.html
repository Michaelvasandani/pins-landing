How to download a video with Pins

Four steps. No copying links, no downloader sites. Click a step to jump to it in the video.

  1. 1

    Open Frevana and choose Pin

    Go to the page with the video. Click the Frevana icon in your Chrome toolbar, then choose Pin.

  2. 2

    Click Pin video, then Stop

    Pins finds the video on the page and records it as it plays. Click Stop when you have what you need.

  3. 3

    Open it in My Pins

    Your video is saved to My Pins, the library inside the extension. Pick it to preview it.

  4. 4

    Click Download

    The video is saved to your computer as an MP4, and it stays in My Pins for later.

Why teams use Pins as their video downloader

Most video downloaders make you copy a link, paste it into a website full of ads, and hope the file comes back. Pins works where you already are: on the page, inside Chrome. Spot a video, pin it, and download it without leaving the tab.

Pins is built for marketers, creators and ecommerce teams who collect video references every day. Every download lands in My Pins along with the images, posts and text you save, so nothing gets lost.

Because Pins is part of Frevana, a downloaded video is a starting point. Send it to ReGen or Ad Clone to make an on-brand version for your own product.
